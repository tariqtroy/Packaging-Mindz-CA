ADSENSE SETUP

1. Open adsense-config.js
2. Put your Google AdSense Publisher ID in CLIENT_ID, e.g. ca-pub-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX
3. Put your Ad Unit Slot ID in SLOT_ID.
4. Save and upload the whole folder.

If you use Auto Ads only, CLIENT_ID is enough; SLOT_ID can stay empty.
Ads may take time to appear after AdSense approval/configuration.
